Features

Everything the business needs, in one system.

Clafina is not a collection of tools sold together. It is one set of records with the screens a business actually uses — so the money, the stock and the people never disagree.

Money and books Sell and stock People and work Grow and understand Every surface Safety and control
01 — Money and books

Books that hold, from the first invoice.

Every document posts to a real double-entry ledger. That is true of a cash sale at the till as much as a credit note from the office.

Invoicing

Send an invoice, take a part-payment and chase the rest — with credit notes and write-offs that keep the books straight.

General ledger

Double-entry postings, journals, trial balance and a closed period, as a report rather than a spreadsheet.

Tax and VAT

Rates by country, tax numbers on the document, and the name that country uses for the tax.

Payables and expenses

Record a bill, claim an expense from a receipt photo, approve it, then pay it in a run.

Budgets and cost centres

Give a project, campaign or department its own budget and watch it spent against the actual.

Multi-currency

Hold balances in more than one currency, with the rates and revaluation kept alongside.

Wallets

Fund, move and convert money between wallets. Every movement is a balanced posting, and the books close to the kobo.

Intercompany

Several companies under one group, with intercompany balances and a consolidation view.

Banks and reconciliation

Bank accounts, statements and a reconciliation that shows exactly what does not match.

02 — Sell and stock

One stock list, everywhere it is sold.

The shop, the counter and the marketplaces draw on the same catalogue and the same stock. A sale in one place is a fact in the others.

Catalogue

Products, variants, units, barcodes and pictures — with the quantity steps each unit sells in.

Price lists

Different prices for different customers, with discounts that follow rules instead of memory.

Stock

Levels per location, movements, adjustments and transfers, each with a history you can read.

Quotation to invoice

Quote, order, deliver and invoice. Every document stands on its own, and conversion only pre-fills.

Purchasing

Purchase orders, goods received notes and supplier bills that match what arrived.

Point of sale

Tills that sell through a dropped connection, shifts that add up, and exceptions a manager resolves in the office.

Online shop

Your own address, theme, pages, currencies and languages — priced and stocked by the business.

Marketplaces

Sell through channels without a second stock list or a nightly export that drifts.

Delivery

Zones and rates, carrier prices and collection points, offered at checkout and priced by the business.

03 — People and work

The people who sell also get paid here.

Customers, staff, suppliers and partners share one directory. So an approval knows who asked, and a document knows who signed.

Customers

One page per customer: documents, balance, activity, and what they owe today.

Suppliers

A portal for suppliers to send bills and see what is owed, without a shared spreadsheet.

Staff

Records, contracts, leave, attendance and what each person is paid — with the history kept.

Projects

Work broken into tasks, with time, cost and billing tracked against it.

Approvals

Requests routed to the person who can decide, with the reason and the decision kept.

Documents

Files, folders, versions and signatures — stored where the record can find them.

Calendar

One calendar for people, rooms and resources, so a booking cannot be made twice.

Messaging

Conversations with customers and between colleagues, kept on the record they concern.

Support

A help desk with service levels, saved replies, a knowledge base and a satisfaction score.

04 — Grow and understand

Answers from the records you already keep.

Because everything is one system, a report is a read — not a project to assemble data for.

Dashboards

Each person's home shows what their job needs, rather than one generic chart for everyone.

Reports

Reports across every module — scheduled, exported, or asked for in words.

Outreach

Email, SMS and WhatsApp campaigns to customers you already have, with the consent they gave.

Loyalty

Points, memberships and gift cards that work at the till and in the shop alike.

Business intelligence

Analytics over live records, with snapshots where a frozen figure is the honest answer.

AI assistant

Ask in words and get an answer from your own documents and records — and a draft only when you confirm it.

05 — Every surface

Six ways in. One source of truth.

Each surface is a client of the same system. Nothing is synced between them, because there is nothing to sync.

Web app

The whole business in a browser — a quotation in the morning, a trial balance in the afternoon. Role-based, keyboard-friendly, and built to be read on a laptop.

Phone app

The same business in your pocket: approvals in one tap, sales documents, customers, money and a payment run, for when you are not at a desk.

Till

Point of sale that keeps selling without a network and settles the day's cash exactly. A sale that arrives late still posts; the office is told what disagreed.

Online shop

A storefront at the organisation's own address, with its catalogue, prices and stock — and checkout in a single request that cannot double-charge.

Wallets

Multi-currency wallets holding real money, funded through banks and providers, with transfers, conversion and payouts — all on double-entry books.

AI

Reads bills and receipts, learns the coding your people confirm, drafts documents with your own tools and answers from your documents — never acting without a person's confirmation.

06 — Safety and control

What protects the records.

The controls are part of the system, not a policy document bolted on afterwards.

Isolated by organisation

Every record carries its organisation and every query is scoped to it. Access is enforced in one place per module, so a new screen cannot forget.

Roles and permissions

People are given exactly the access their job needs, including approving what moves money. Sensitive acts can require a second person.

An audit trail

Who changed what, when, and from where — kept with the record. Nothing important is silently edited.

Nothing is deleted

Records are retired or soft-deleted, never dropped. A mistake is corrected by a new document that says so.
