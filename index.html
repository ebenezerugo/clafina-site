Everything already joined up

Four parts of the business, one set of records.

Most tools are good at one of these and hand the rest to a spreadsheet. Clafina was built as one system, so a sale moves the stock, the ledger and the customer's balance at once.

Money and books

Every document posts to a real ledger, with tax by country and a balance you can trust at any moment.

  • Invoices, bills, payments and credit notes
  • General ledger, trial balance and journals
  • VAT and tax rules per country
  • Budgets, cost centres and projects
  • Expenses, payables and approvals
  • Multi-currency and intercompany

Sell and stock

From quotation to delivery, the stock and the money move together — in the shop, at the counter or through the marketplace.

  • Catalogue, variants and price lists
  • Stock levels, movements and planning
  • Quotations, orders, invoices, waybills
  • Purchasing, suppliers and goods received
  • Point of sale and cash shifts
  • Online shop and marketplace channels

People and work

The same people who sell also get paid, request leave and sign documents. One directory, one calendar, one audit trail.

  • Customers, suppliers and contacts
  • Staff records, leave and attendance
  • Projects, tasks and timesheets
  • Approvals that someone can act on
  • Documents, signatures and files
  • Calendar, meetings and messaging

  • Dashboards for each person's job

Built for the counter

A sale is never lost, and never counted twice.

The till keeps selling when the connection drops. Its journal arrives later, in order, and a sale sent twice changes nothing. When the office disagrees — oversold, price changed — the sale still posts and the disagreement is reported in plain words for a manager.

  • Works with no network, syncs when it returns
  • Cash and shifts add up to the last kobo
  • Every exception is kept, never dropped

Safe to run the business on

Built for the things you cannot lose.

Money, stock and customer details are the records a business cannot reconstruct. Clafina treats them that way.

Isolated by organisation

Every record is scoped to one organisation. One business never sees another's data, by design rather than by filter.

Access you can explain

Roles and permissions decide who may see, change and approve. Sensitive acts can require a second person.

An audit trail

Who changed what, and when — kept with the record, not reconstructed afterwards.

Any country, any currency

Regions, countries and currencies are configuration. Tax rules, addresses and languages follow the organisation.

Accessible by default

The screens are built to WCAG AA, with contrast, focus and keyboard use checked as part of the work.

Your data comes back

Records can be exported, and an organisation can be closed and erased on request. Nothing is held hostage.
