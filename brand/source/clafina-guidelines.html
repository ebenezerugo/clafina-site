01  /  The idea
CLAFINA / 02

One open line.
One clear view.

Clafina is the first letter of the name and the shape of the promise: an open system that holds the whole business in one continuous line.

The C never closes. There is always another sale, another hire, another channel or another idea to take in. The opening faces forward — the direction the business is going.

At the centre sits one citron diamond: the one record. Every surface reads and writes the same numbers, so the picture never splits.

Open
The C is a line, not a wall. The platform takes the next thing in.
Clear
The opening is also an eye. One view, not nine dashboards.
Human
Bone, not grey. A business tool that feels made for people.

Say it plainly.

Short sentences. Concrete nouns. Numbers where they help. “Know what is owed.” not “leverage actionable insights.”

Warm, not cute.

Talk to a person who is busy. No exclamation marks, no jargon, no “revolutionary”. “This is yours to export.”

One claim, then proof.

Every promise gets a number, a screenshot or a customer. “Close the books in a day.” “World-class.”

One clear view. One record.
