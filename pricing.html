Questions

The things people ask first.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes, for one person and one organisation, with the core features and no time limit and no card. It is the same product, not a demo — when you add a second person you choose a paid plan.

What counts as a person?

Anyone who signs in. Staff who only appear on documents do not, and neither do your customers or suppliers.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

Nothing is deleted on the day a payment is missed. You are reminded, then given notice, then suspended — and everything is still there, and exportable, when you come back.

Can I use only the till, or only the online shop?

Yes. Each surface is a client of the same system, so you can start with one and switch the others on later without moving any data.

Do you support more than one currency and country?

Yes. Countries, currencies, tax rules and languages are configuration per organisation, and a group can hold companies in different currencies.

How do payments work?

Card and transfer collection runs through a payment provider the organisation connects and controls. Cash, bank transfer and cheque can be recorded by hand from day one.

Can I move my data in from another system?

Yes — catalogues, customers and opening balances are imported from a spreadsheet, and we will help with the first one. Nothing is locked to a format you cannot get back out.
