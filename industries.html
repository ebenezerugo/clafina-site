Industries

The core is shared. The trade is yours.

Every business needs books, stock, people and customers. A trade needs a few more things — a floor plan, a scouting sheet, a work order. Clafina ships those as packs on the same core, so nothing is a separate system.

Retail Restaurant Farm Property Construction Manufacturing Accommodation Field service Courses Fundraising
Retail

Sell in the shop, online and at the counter — from one stock list.

Catalogue with variants and units, price lists per customer, stock per location, a till that sells offline, and gift cards and loyalty that work everywhere.

  • Tills with offline journal and cash shifts
  • Online shop at your own address
  • Returns, exchanges and gift cards
Restaurant

The floor, the kitchen and the till on one screen each.

Tables and covers, a menu with combos and modifiers, a kitchen display, tips, waste and a service report that matches the cash drawer.

  • Floor plan, tables and course timing
  • Menu, combos and item availability
  • Tips, waste and end-of-shift cash
Farm

Inputs, scouts and yields, recorded where the work happens.

Applications logged against a field, scouts assigned and their findings captured, product profiles for each crop, and the cost of every input carried into the books.

  • Field and plot records with applications
  • Scouting rounds, assignments and findings
  • Input costs and yield against plan
Property

Units, tenants and rent without a separate ledger.

Properties and units, leases with their terms, rent invoiced on schedule, service charges and maintenance requests tracked to the unit that caused them.

  • Properties, units and occupancy
  • Leases, rent and service charges
  • Maintenance and work orders per unit
Construction

Sites, bills of quantities and progress you can invoice.

Projects broken into sites and phases, materials issued to a site, subcontractor bills, and a valuation that turns work done into an invoice.

  • Projects, phases and milestones
  • Materials, plant and subcontractors
  • Progress valuations and retention
Manufacturing

From bill of materials to the finished cost.

Bills of materials and routings, work orders on the shopfloor, quality checks at each step, and a costing that follows the actual material and time used.

  • Bills of materials and routings
  • Work orders, shopfloor and quality
  • Material, labour and overhead costing
Accommodation

Rooms, rates and bookings that cannot double-book.

Room types and rates, availability across the calendar, bookings with deposits, housekeeping status and the guest's bill settled to the folio.

  • Room types, rates and seasons
  • Bookings, deposits and folios
  • Housekeeping and maintenance
Field service

Jobs, routes and a signature at the door.

Work orders scheduled to a person and a place, checklists completed on the phone, parts used from stock, and a signature that closes the job and bills it.

  • Scheduling across people and sites
  • Checklists and parts on the phone
  • Sign-off, photos and invoicing
Courses and classes

Enrolment, attendance and fees in one place.

Courses and classes with their timetable, enrolment and waiting lists, attendance, fees invoiced per term, and a record a parent or student can be sent.

  • Courses, classes and timetables
  • Enrolment, waiting lists and attendance
  • Fees, discounts and statements
Fundraising

Donors, campaigns and the receipt they expect.

Donor records with their history, campaigns with a target, pledges paid over time, grants with their reporting dates, and a receipt issued the moment money arrives.

  • Donors, pledges and recurring giving
  • Campaigns, targets and grants
  • Receipts and restricted funds
